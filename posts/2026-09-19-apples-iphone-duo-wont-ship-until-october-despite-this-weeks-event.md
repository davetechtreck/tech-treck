title: Apple's iPhone Duo Won't Ship Until October Despite This Week's Event
date: 2026-09-19
tags: apple, iphone, foldable
snippet: The foldable iPhone was announced alongside the 18 Pro, but you can't actually buy it yet.
---

Apple unveiled its first foldable iPhone at its September 9 event this week, but if you were planning to get your hands on one immediately, you're out of luck. According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, the device—officially called the [iPhone Duo](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=iPhone+Duo&tag=techtreck02-20)—will go on sale as early as October, not alongside the [iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=iPhone+18+Pro&tag=techtreck02-20) models that were announced at the same event.

The staggered launch is notable because Apple typically ships flagship products within a week or two of announcement. The iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max are already available for pre-order with standard September ship dates, while the Duo gets pushed to next month at minimum. That "as early as October" phrasing suggests even that timeline isn't locked in.

It's a familiar playbook. Apple did something similar with the iPhone X back in 2017, announcing it alongside the iPhone 8 but delaying shipments by several weeks. The difference here is that foldables are notoriously difficult to manufacture at scale, and Apple is entering a market where Samsung, Motorola, and others have been iterating for years. A delay to get the hinge mechanism and display durability right makes sense, even if it's frustrating for early adopters.

The iPhone Duo joins a crowded September lineup that includes the iPhone 18 Pro, [iPhone 18 Pro Max](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=iPhone+18+Pro+Max&tag=techtreck02-20), [Apple Watch Series 12](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Apple+Watch+Series+12&tag=techtreck02-20), [Apple Watch Ultra 4](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Apple+Watch+Ultra+4&tag=techtreck02-20), and [AirPods 5](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=AirPods+5&tag=techtreck02-20). The [Mac Studio](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mac+Studio&tag=techtreck02-20) with M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips also started shipping on September 22 with prices beginning at $2,499, though the 512GB unified memory configuration won't arrive until late October.

For context, Apple isn't releasing a standard iPhone 18 this year. The company is focusing its September lineup exclusively on premium models, leaving the base-tier iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 pushed to early 2027. That makes the Duo delay even more conspicuous—Apple's most experimental device of the year is also the one customers will have to wait longest for.

The October ship date means Apple will collect pre-orders and build hype through the end of September without actually delivering hardware. Whether that's a deliberate scarcity play or a genuine production constraint, the message is clear: if you want Apple's first foldable, patience is required.
