Razer is backing Microsoft's 25th-anniversary celebration with translucent green versions of three of its most popular gaming peripherals. All three ship October 20, and they're already available for pre-order.

The [Razer Basilisk V3 Pro 35K – Xbox 25th Anniversary Edition](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Razer+Basilisk+V3+Pro+35K+%E2%80%93+Xbox+25th+Anniversary+Edition&tag=techtreck02-20) is an ergonomic wireless gaming mouse with the Focus Pro 35K Optical Sensor Gen-2, a HyperScroll Tilt Wheel, and 13 customizable controls. It costs $179.99. The mouse supports HyperSpeed Wireless connectivity and standard Razer RGB lighting.

The [Razer BlackWidow V4 75% – Xbox 25th Anniversary Edition](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Razer+BlackWidow+V4+75%25+%E2%80%93+Xbox+25th+Anniversary+Edition&tag=techtreck02-20) is a compact mechanical keyboard with hot-swappable keys and Razer Orange Tactile Switches Gen-3. Its key feature is a dedicated button that launches the Xbox Game Bar directly from the keyboard. It carries per-key Chroma RGB lighting and comes with a magnetic plush wrist rest. The keyboard is priced at $209.99.

The [Razer Hammerhead V3 X HyperSpeed for Xbox – Xbox 25th Anniversary Edition](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Razer+Hammerhead+V3+X+HyperSpeed+for+Xbox+%E2%80%93+Xbox+25th+Anniversary+Edition&tag=techtreck02-20) is a pair of wireless earbuds with HyperSpeed Wireless 2.4 GHz connectivity for low-latency gaming across PC, console, and mobile. They offer Bluetooth 5.3, IPX4 water resistance, and up to 35 hours of battery life including the charging case. The earbuds cost $119.99.

All three products use a see-through green shell matching the aesthetic of the original 2001 Xbox, complete with a 25th-anniversary logo on each device. Razer notes that pre-orders opened August 25, and the collection will be on display at gamescom 2026. This is a limited-edition run, so availability may be tight once October 20 arrives.

If you're a collector or built your gaming setup around Razer gear, the anniversary styling makes these worth considering. The price premiums are modest—roughly $20 more than their standard versions—and the dedicated Xbox button on the keyboard is a practical addition for Game Bar quick access. For casual players, the individual pieces work fine as standalone upgrades.
