[Apple TV 4K](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Apple+TV+4K&tag=techtreck02-20) just jumped from $249 to $299, which sounds like a $50 increase until you realize the previous model had a cheaper $199 tier. From that baseline, the new Apple TV 4K costs $100 more. The kicker: there's now only one configuration instead of two, and it ships with 128GB of storage, Wi-Fi 7, and Ethernet.

The hardware improvements are real. Apple upgraded to the A19 chip, promising up to 50 percent faster CPU performance and up to twice the GPU performance compared with the previous generation. There's a dedicated AV1 decoder for more efficient video streaming. Built-in microphones now enable hands-free Siri AI commands. The redesigned [Siri Remote](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Siri+Remote&tag=techtreck02-20) can play an audio chime to help you locate it between couch cushions—a small feature that shouldn't need celebrating in a $300 device.

But none of this adds up to a $100 jump. A19 performance gains matter for gaming and local processing, but most Apple TV 4K buyers are streaming Netflix and Apple TV+, not rendering 3D graphics. The microphones and Siri voice control are nice additions, but they're not premium features. Wall mounting support for HomePod speakers in surround setups? That's ecosystem integration, not a spec upgrade.

The real issue is configuration strategy. Killing the $199 entry tier removes choice. Forcing everyone into a single 128GB SKU at $299 means there's no option for price-conscious buyers anymore. If you wanted an Apple TV 4K last year, you could pick a cheaper box or spend more for better storage. Now you take what Apple gives you.

This lands wrong today because Apple just announced [HomeView](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HomeView&tag=techtreck02-20), its first smart home hub with a 7-inch Liquid Retina display. HomeView also costs $299. So for the same price, you can buy either a streaming device or an entirely new category of smart home control center. That's not a coincidence—it's a choice customers shouldn't have to make.

Pre-orders are open today. Apple TV 4K ships November 13.
