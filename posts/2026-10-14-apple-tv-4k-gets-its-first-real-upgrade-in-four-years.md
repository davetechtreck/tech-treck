Apple waited four years to refresh the [Apple TV 4K](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Apple+TV+4K&tag=techtreck02-20), and when it finally arrived on October 13, the hardware didn't look different. But the guts did. The new fourth-generation Apple TV 4K packs an A19 chip—the same processor Apple dropped into the iPhone 17 last year—with 50% faster CPU performance and double the GPU power compared to the aging A15 inside the 2022 model.

That's not the story, though. The real story is that Apple built this box around Siri AI. The new Siri is generative, context-aware, and tied to your Apple account and devices. It works hands-free through three far-field microphones baked into the box. You can ask it questions, control your home theater, and get personalized responses based on what's on your iPhone. It's the first time Apple's next-generation Siri shows up anywhere—not on phones, not on watches, but on a $299 streaming device.

The price is the problem. Apple TV 4K in 2022 started at $129 with Wi-Fi only and 64GB storage. Apple raised it twice: once in June to $199, citing memory costs from AI data centers. Now it's $299, and there's only one model—128GB with Wi-Fi 7, Gigabit Ethernet, and nothing cheaper. That's a $170 jump from the original 2022 price, and a $100 leap from where the previous model sat just four months ago.

Apple is trying to justify the jump. Wi-Fi 7, AV1 video decoding, Bluetooth 6, HDMI 2.1, Thread networking support for smart home gear—it's a solid spec bump. The redesigned [Siri Remote](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Siri+Remote&tag=techtreck02-20) now has a physical locator button that plays a sound so you can find it in the couch. You can pair up to four HomePod speakers for surround sound, something the old box couldn't do.

But here's the thing: if you already have an Apple TV 4K and you mainly stream movies and shows, the upgrade doesn't change what you see. A 4K stream looks identical on both boxes. The A19 handles everything smoothly. Siri AI isn't available in every region yet—only English for launch, and not in the EU at all. If you want that new remote, you can buy it standalone for $59.

The Apple TV 4K (2026) launches November 13 for preorder now. It's worth buying if you live in the Apple ecosystem, use HomeKit, and want hands-free control of your entire setup. If streaming is all you do, the calculus is harder. Apple is asking you to pay for the future of TV, which is increasingly AI-centered and expensive.
